---
title: Balancing Act
date: 2026-02-18
---

the slides on the playground only go up
so everyone stays on the ground;
ants in a chaotic line
wander back to their hidden Queen
with news of food, and more food.

the first step is the only one;
your direction changes as gravity steals your fate
from beneath your feet, suddenly weightless like a leaf
in a spring breeze, almost forgetting the branch
you still cling to, gray

steel bars and cold cement solicit your return from the escapade,
streetlights—your firmament, dear flat-earther—blinking
in imitation faerie lights you thought belonged to Her.
rising, you expound your tale to those ant friends you made,
they listen and nod and say oh that’s nice, why don’t you join us for lunch.

so you do, you poor little fool, swayed
by their breath, Her breath, humming through the silicon hill,
now telling you whom you have squished
by falling haphazaradly into your ranks,
and your only defense is a memory and a trinket.

the slides on the playground only go up
and everyone’s stuck on the ground;
ants in a perfect line
march back to their hidden Queen
with news of food, and only food.
