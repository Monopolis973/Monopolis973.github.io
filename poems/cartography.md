---
title: Cartography
date: 2024-04-19
---

Every city I've lived in
exists in two versions:
the one the maps agree on,
and the one I knew.

Mine has a diner that closed in 2019
still open on a corner,
a bike lane that smelled like rain,
a particular window I passed
that was always lit at midnight
and I never found out why.

If I went back now
I would navigate by the ghost.
Turn left at the place I used to
feel a certain way.
Continue past the memory
of a year when things were different.

The official map would get me there.
But my map is the one I live in.
