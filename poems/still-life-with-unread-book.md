---
title: Still Life with Unread Book
date: 2024-01-08
---

It sits on the nightstand where I put it
three weeks ago, spine uncracked,
a rectangle of promise
I keep rotating toward.

The lamp beside it knows its title better than I do —
has read the cover in yellow light
a hundred times
while I have looked at my phone.

I mean to. That's the thing.
Every night I mean to,
and every night the ceiling
is more interesting than I expected.

Tomorrow I will read it.
Tomorrow is a country
I move toward all my life
and never quite arrive.
