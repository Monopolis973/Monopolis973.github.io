Changing your mind should feel like something. Here are a few cases where it has, for me.

## On Productivity

I used to believe that the right system — the right app, the right framework, the right ritual — could unlock something. That the bottleneck was organization.

I no longer believe this. The bottleneck is almost always motivation, which is to say, caring. No system survives contact with not-caring.

## On Having Strong Opinions

I used to prize holding strong opinions early, treating it as a form of intellectual courage. The world presented a question; I would form a view before other people had the nerve to.

What I missed: the people I most admire are slow to reach conclusions, and it shows in the quality of what they say when they do. The patience wasn't timidity — it was precision.

## On What Other People Think

This one is ongoing. I notice I have more freedom than I once did, and I'm not sure what to attribute it to. Maybe just age.
